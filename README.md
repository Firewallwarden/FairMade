**Fair pricing. Sustainable businesses. Confident buying.**

FairMade is an AI-powered marketplace and business intelligence platform designed to promote fair, transparent, and sustainable commerce between independent sellers and consumers.

The platform empowers small businesses, farmers, livestock producers, artisans, and local makers to understand their true production costs, optimize pricing, evaluate suppliers, manage inventory, and make informed business decisions without compromising product quality.

For buyers, FairMade provides a transparent shopping experience through local seller discovery, product comparisons, and AI-assisted insights into product pricing, quality, and overall value.

At the heart of FairMade is the **Fair Price Engine**, an intelligent pricing system that evaluates production economics, local market conditions, and seller-defined quality standards to recommend sustainable price ranges. Its companion feature, the **Fair Price Passport**, helps consumers understand the factors behind a product's price while respecting sellers' confidential business information.

Integrated with **PayPal**, FairMade connects intelligent pricing and business analytics with secure digital commerce, helping sellers build sustainable businesses and buyers make confident purchasing decisions.

**Our mission is to make commerce fairer for everyone—ensuring sellers are compensated appropriately for their work while buyers receive transparency, quality, and meaningful value.**
